  - type: text
    eyebrow: Current Strataline explanation
    heading: Painted popcorn is a different problem from unpainted texture
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Traditional unpainted texture can sometimes be misted or wetted so it softens enough for controlled hand scraping. Once the texture has been painted, moisture penetration can be greatly reduced. Scraping may still be possible, but it can become much slower and can leave an uneven substrate that needs further correction.
      - >-
        The historical alternative was often repeated wetting and hand scraping with a broad knife. That approach could create sticky debris, require extensive protection and move the cleanup burden around the room. The right method depends on the coating, texture bond and underlying ceiling; this project demonstrated why painted popcorn should be assessed separately instead of treated as ordinary unpainted texture.

  - type: text
    eyebrow: Current Strataline explanation
    heading: This project is where our dust-controlled process matured
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The process combined power sanding with a vacuum connection at the tool, source extraction, plastic containment, an appropriate abrasive and a controlled sanding technique. On this project, the system included a Festool Planex connected to a Festool dust extractor. Capturing material where the abrasive met the ceiling reduced the amount available to travel through the condo.
      - >-
        Mechanical removal was not the finish line. Once the texture was removed, the exposed ceiling still had to be checked, corrected and skim coated where required before primer and paint. Pot-light locations also had to be coordinated before the final finish so completed surfaces would not be reopened later.
      - >-
        The Toronto Star reported that Strataline had invested about $5,000 in specialized sanding and extraction equipment for this type of work—significant investment for a highly specialized renovation process in 2017. The equipment supported dust control; containment, technique and disciplined cleanup completed the system. Dust-controlled is the accurate technical description, not a claim of literal zero airborne particles.
