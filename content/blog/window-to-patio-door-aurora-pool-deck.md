    eyebrow: Starting with an existing window
    heading: A window and a door are very different openings.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        A window-to-patio-door conversion is not simply a matter of removing one unit and substituting another. The opening has to be evaluated as a new exterior doorway, including the surrounding structure, weatherproofing and threshold conditions. The work may involve enlarging the wall opening, modifying interior finishes, resolving the exterior brick or cladding, preparing the sill area and coordinating the new doorway with the existing deck elevation.
      - >-
        Those are planning considerations, not a claim that every possible framing or masonry operation occurred here. The photographs show the original window, the enlarged opening during construction and the completed door, but they do not establish concealed header details or a particular structural method. Project records would be needed before making those claims.
      - >-
        Converting a window into a door can affect the building envelope and structural opening, and municipal permit requirements should be confirmed before construction. This project story does not state whether a permit was or was not required or obtained for this specific home.

    eyebrow: Door and threshold integration
    heading: The threshold matters as much as the door.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        A patio-door opening has to work at floor level, where the interior flooring, door sill, exterior deck surface, drainage and weather protection meet. These conditions should be considered together before the opening is created. A door that looks correct higher in the wall can still be awkward if the sill and landing relationship are treated as afterthoughts.
      - >-
        Here, the finished exterior photographs show the door opening directly onto the existing deck. We are not assigning an exact height difference or concealed waterproofing specification from the images. The supportable point is that the door, sill, brick perimeter and deck connection form one finished transition between the bedroom and backyard.

    listHeading: Questions to resolve before construction
    listItems:
      - Confirm whether the wall opening can be enlarged as intended.
      - Check municipal and building requirements before construction.
      - Plan structural, exterior and interior work as one scope.
      - Consider the threshold height and existing deck or landing.
      - Treat exterior waterproofing as functional work, not a cosmetic detail.
      - Match the new opening carefully to the existing brick or cladding.
      - Reconsider furniture placement and interior circulation once the window becomes a doorway.
      - Recognize that a focused new opening can improve usability more than a much larger renovation.
