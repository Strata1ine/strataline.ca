    listHeading: Planning lessons from the project
    listItems:
      - Dust-heavy work is easier to contain and extract when sanding stages are planned together.
      - Popcorn removal belongs before newly finished floors and stairs become harder to protect.
      - Smooth ceilings may require several skim-and-sand correction passes before paint.
      - New hardwood changes how adjoining nosing, stairs and railings should be finished.
      - Upper-floor nosing is part of the floor-and-stair transition, not an afterthought.
      - One contractor coordinating connected scopes reduces sequencing conflicts between trades.
