Good painting preparation starts with decisions, not drop cloths. Before work begins, the homeowner and contractor should agree on the rooms, surfaces, repairs, colours and handling of furniture or fixtures.

## Confirm every surface in the scope

List walls, ceilings, trim, doors, closets and built-ins separately. Do not assume that “paint the room” includes every connected surface.

## Review repairs in normal lighting

Mark cracks, dents, old anchors, failed caulking and previous patches. Repair expectations should be clear before the finish colour is applied, especially where daylight or wall lighting exposes surface variation.

## Create practical access

Small items, art and personal belongings should be removed from the work area. Agree in advance on who moves heavy furniture, window coverings, electronics and fragile objects.

## Confirm colours and sheen

Record the colour and sheen for each surface. Existing colour, lighting and surrounding finishes can change how a sample reads, so review representative samples in the actual room.

## Plan occupied-home logistics

Identify the order of rooms, daily access, pets, ventilation needs and where protected household items will be stored. A clear sequence reduces repeated moving and helps keep finished rooms separate from active work.
