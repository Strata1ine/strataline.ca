    eyebrow: Why the carpet had to go
    heading: The carpet was already gone when we arrived.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The homeowners did not need convincing that the old carpet had reached the end of its life. The house was approximately 20 years old, and years of foot traffic had left the stair covering worn and holding accumulated household dust. One homeowner reported that dust from the worn carpet was aggravating her allergies, so the carpet was removed before the renovation began. That is the client's account of the existing condition, not a medical claim about the renovation.
      - >-
        Once the carpet was gone, the original staircase structure remained along with a practical decision: install another soft covering, or convert the visible stair surfaces permanently to finished hardwood. The owners wanted something durable and cleanable, without recreating the same fibrous surface they had just removed. They chose oak stair capping and a coordinated railing renovation.

    eyebrow: Why hard-surface stairs made sense
    heading: The staircase itself was sound. The finishes were the problem.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Stair capping is appropriate when the underlying staircase can remain and the goal is to change the durable, visible surfaces without rebuilding sound structure unnecessarily. This was not a structurally complicated staircase. Its existing geometry could be retained where suitable, while oak caps were fitted to the prepared steps, the risers and stringers were finished white, and the railing system was modernized around the new stair finish.
      - >-
        The result does not read as carpet removed from an unfinished set of steps. The oak nosings, fitted winders, painted vertical surfaces and coordinated railing make it read as a complete hardwood staircase. That distinction depends on preparation and finish carpentry, not simply laying wood over whatever is underneath.

    eyebrow: Preparing the existing staircase
    heading: Capping starts with the structure that is already there.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Removing carpet exposes the staircase, but it does not automatically make the surface ready for hardwood. The substrate has to be checked, fasteners and residue dealt with, and every tread location prepared so the cap can sit properly. The existing stair geometry also establishes the rise, run and direction of each new visible piece; capping cannot be used to disguise an unsuitable or unstable base.
      - >-
        On this Richmond Hill project, the original staircase could remain. The execution challenge was to fit the new oak accurately through the straight steps and changing winder shapes, then coordinate the nosings with crisp white risers and stringers. Once the vertical surfaces become white, inconsistent lines and poorly fitted joints become easier—not harder—to see.
  - type: text
    eyebrow: Winder fitting
    heading: The winders are where precise stair capping becomes obvious.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Straight rectangular treads are comparatively simple. Winders change shape as the stair turns, and each piece must respond to the wall, stringer and adjacent tread rather than to one repeated rectangle. Existing houses are not assumed to be perfectly square, so every winder is fitted to its actual location.
      - >-
        The nosing lines still need to read consistently as the direction changes. Gaps that might be less noticeable against a dark surface become obvious beside white risers and stringers. The finished detail below shows the individually shaped oak pieces following the turn while keeping the visible edges deliberate and the painted intersections clean.

    eyebrow: Dust control and finishing
    heading: A cleaner staircase was part of the reason for doing the renovation.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Creating a finished hardwood staircase while filling the house with uncontrolled sanding dust would have worked against an important part of the homeowners' objective. Dust-producing preparation was organized around protection, containment and professional extraction connected to sanding tools where applicable. Work areas and materials were kept orderly, and cleanup was treated as part of the sequence rather than left until every operation was complete.
      - >-
        The stain and protective clear finish also had to be practical inside an occupied home. Waterborne stains and clear finishes were chosen to minimize strong solvent odour and VOC exposure while allowing the oak colour and surrounding floor to be coordinated. This is deliberately not a “zero VOC” claim, and dust-controlled does not mean that renovation work can be guaranteed to create no dust whatsoever.
      - >-
        The homeowner reported being very satisfied with the working conditions and the finished project. That feedback is presented as her experience of the renovation, not as evidence that stair work treated or cured an allergy.

    heading: The staircase and flooring had to share a colour relationship.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        A staircase is rarely seen in isolation. At the lower landing, the new oak sits directly beside surrounding hardwood, so stain decisions had to be judged as a transition rather than as a separate sample. The goal was coordination, not the false promise that different boards, grain patterns and lighting angles would become visually identical.

Hardwood is not allergen-free, but it gives the homeowners an accessible, non-fibrous surface to maintain.

        The most useful lesson is that removing worn carpet does not automatically require rebuilding an entire staircase. When the existing structure is sound and suitable, precise capping can permanently change the visible material while retaining the underlying geometry.
    listHeading: Planning lessons from the project
    listItems:
      - A sound existing staircase can often be transformed through hardwood capping rather than complete reconstruction.
      - Winders require individual fitting because their shapes and surrounding geometry change through the turn.
      - Railing design strongly affects whether a converted staircase looks genuinely renovated.
      - White risers and stringers expose poor joints, making accurate finish carpentry especially important.
      - Dust control matters when indoor cleanliness is one of the reasons for removing carpet.
      - Waterborne finishing systems can reduce the strong solvent odours associated with older finishing methods.
      - Stair and flooring stain decisions should be evaluated together in the home.
