    heading: Why planning mattered
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        The original basement was open and unfinished, with exposed concrete walls, visible services and a slab that did not provide a ready surface for the planned flooring. That openness made the available area easy to see, but it also made the central design problem clear: every new wall could make the basement feel smaller.
      - >-
        The finished program was substantial for approximately 800 square feet. It needed a living room, bedroom, custom kitchen, separate laundry room, bathroom with walk-in shower, large closet and a useful bedroom work surface. The oak staircase, windows and walkout also had to feel connected to the completed space rather than treated as leftover conditions.

  - eyebrow: PLANNING
    heading: The basement was designed before it was built.
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        Multiple layouts were explored before construction. The planning work tested circulation between the staircase, living area, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, laundry and walkout. It also made it possible to check kitchen proportions, furniture zones, storage, door swings and the amount of open space that would remain once the supporting rooms were defined.
      - >-
        A floor plan tells you whether everything technically fits. A 3D model lets you experience the proportions before the walls exist. In this project, the model made it possible to move through the proposed basement virtually, look in different directions and judge whether the rooms felt right before construction started—almost like walking through a video-game environment that had not yet been built.

    heading: Built-in function instead of more furniture

      - >-

Mirrored sliding closet doors provide access to substantial storage without a hinged door projecting into the room. A built-in desk or work surface occupies the adjacent recess, with a power outlet positioned for that use.
      - >-
        With the storage and work surface already integrated, the room can function with a bed and chair rather than requiring separate wardrobes, shelving and a desk. The layout also gives the basement a high degree of independence from the main floor.

    heading: What this project shows about planning an 800 sq. ft. basement
    paragraphs:
      - >-
        A successful compact basement is not created by fitting rooms one at a time. Each decision changes circulation, storage and the amount of shared space that remains. Testing the whole plan before framing makes those trade-offs visible while they are still comparatively easy to change.
    listItems:

      - Custom cabinetry can make better use of constrained walls than standard furniture.
      - Built-ins can reduce the amount of furniture a small bedroom needs.
      - Keep the strongest windows and walkout connected to the main living area where practical.
      - Bathrooms, laundry and storage should be planned as part of the entire floor plan—not inserted afterward.
      - Stair finishing matters because the staircase is the visual transition between the main home and basement.
